✨ Load 8 Protocol & Real Budget
An executive-grade, psychodynamic life and financial operating system designed for high-demand professionals.

Load 8 Protocol bridges the gap between internal cognitive load and external financial behavior. Built to prevent burnout before it impacts decision-making, this system tracks psychodynamic stress metrics in real-time and dynamically regulates spending boundaries based on your current operational capacity.

🚀 Key Features
📊 Daily Command Center (Psychodynamic Tracking): Measures internal load across Work, Cognitive, Emotional, Survival, and Recovery dimensions. Automatically computes Net Strain and enforces Ego Operational Modes (Green, Yellow, Red).

🛡️ Automated Protection Modes & Budget Freezes: When strain crosses safe thresholds (Red Mode), discretionary spending categories are automatically frozen, and a pre-approved safety buffer is instanced to protect the operator.

💳 Adaptive Financial Budgeting: Dynamic spending caps that adapt in real-time based on your primary overarching goal (e.g., Debt Momentum, Retirement Wealth, Emergency Buffer).

📈 7-Day Calibration & Analytics: Rolling 7-day metric analysis, trend visualization charts, category burn-rate tracking, and automated 2-day low-recovery thaw logging.

🧘‍♂️ Local AI Recovery Coach: Integrated offline AI coaching powered by Ollama, reading live session state (Net Strain, Ego Mode, and active goals) to deliver zero-shame, grounding, nervous-system-regulated guidance entirely on your local hardware.

🛠️ Tech Stack
Frontend & UI: Streamlit (Python) featuring custom CSS styled with a Midnight Obsidian (#0B0C10) and Luminous Gold (#D4AF37) executive aesthetic.

Data Engine: Pandas for real-time DataFrame aggregation, rolling metrics, and state management.

AI Backend: Ollama (running locally via custom model weights) for 100% private, offline executive coaching.
